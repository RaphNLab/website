Fire & gas detection system

Embedded engineering · Cameroon · In development

01 / Challenge

Fires destroyed hundreds of stalls in Cameroon's major markets.

02 / Constraints

  • Low cost per node, so a whole market can be covered
  • No reliance on cellular coverage
  • Low power, unattended operation
  • Detect both smoke/gas and rising temperature

03 / Architecture

  1. MQ-2 + T°
  2. Cortex-M0+
  3. LoRa
  4. Gateway
  5. Alert

04 / Engineering decisions

  • LoRa: long range at low power, independent of mobile networks.
  • Cortex-M0+: low cost and low power, enough headroom for sensor processing.
  • Two sensing channels: gas/smoke plus temperature.

05 / Current status

In development: hardware and firmware are being built and tested.

06 / Stack

  • Cortex-M0+
  • C
  • LoRa / LoRaWAN
  • MQ-2
