For companies & institutions

Embedded software engineering, delivered by people who also teach it.

We design and build firmware for ARM Cortex-M systems, architect IoT products end to end, and upskill your team along the way.

What we do

RaphNLab works with companies, research groups and universities who need embedded systems expertise without carrying a full-time hire.

Firmware & embedded software

Bare-metal and RTOS firmware for ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers, from board bring-up to shipped product.

  • Driver development: GPIO, ADC/DAC, I2C, SPI, UART, CAN, DMA
  • FreeRTOS task architecture and real-time scheduling
  • Bootloaders, watchdog-based recovery, low-power modes

IoT & systems architecture

End-to-end architecture for connected products, including long-range, low-connectivity scenarios.

  • LoRa / LoRaWAN connectivity for constrained environments
  • Sensor integration and signal-chain design
  • Embedded Linux for higher-complexity edge devices

Corporate & team training

The same curriculum we sell to individuals, delivered on-site or remotely to upskill an existing team.

  • Custom curriculum built around your product and stack
  • C programming through to microcontroller fundamentals
  • Loaner hardware kits included for the duration of training

Contract & outsourced development

Dedicated execution for a defined scope, delivered against clear milestones.

  • Fixed-scope or time-boxed engagements
  • Clear documentation handed over at the end, not held back

Curriculum partnerships

We design and deliver embedded systems courses for universities, as we did for the IRT programme at Université des Montagnes, Cameroon.

  • Full syllabus design, lab equipment lists, and grading support
  • Delivered by an instructor with real industry experience
