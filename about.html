About us

An embedded systems lab built one workshop at a time.

RaphNLab is a Germany-and-Cameroon embedded systems practice.

Our story

From one engineer's notebook to 213 people trained.

RaphNLab was founded in October 2020 by Silvere Sacker Ngoufack.

Today RaphNLab runs on the same premise it started with.

We work as engineers for hire, and as teachers.

Recognised GIZ Diaspora Expert

Silvere Sacker Ngoufack introducing embedded systems to a mixed-age workshop group, Frankfurt.

Mission

RaphNLab is a research and training practice in embedded systems and applied software engineering.

Vision

We want to build a community of developers working on projects that matter.

49% of international bachelor's students in Germany never finish their degree — nearly double the rate for German students.

What we believe

Four ideas that shape how we work

Knowledge sharing, by default

What we learn on a paid project becomes a free seminar.

Hands-on, always

Every course chapter ships with real exercises on real hardware.

A bridge, not a one-way pipe

Knowledge moves both directions between Germany and Cameroon.

Fair pricing, real access

Courses sustain the lab; kids' workshops stay free.
